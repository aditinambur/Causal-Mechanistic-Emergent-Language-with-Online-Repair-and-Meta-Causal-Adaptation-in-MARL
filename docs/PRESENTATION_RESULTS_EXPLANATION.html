2. Slide 1 Deep Dive: Testing 1 — Communication Utility

Purpose of Testing 1

Before claiming that an AI system can repair broken communication, one must first prove that communication was actually learned and functional in the first place. In cooperative multi-agent RL, agents frequently learn to solve tasks using only physical vision while outputting random tokens. Testing 1 uses Pearl's interventional do-calculus to isolate the pure causal utility of communication.

Protocol Section Breakdown (Line by Line)

  1. 5 independently trained seeds per setting, 500,000 steps each
  2. Plain English: Five separate multi-agent neural network models were initialized with different random seeds and trained from scratch for 500,000 environment interaction steps for each setting (2a3l and 3a4l).

  3. Why it matters: Using 5 independent runs ensures that findings are statistically sound and not the result of a single lucky training run.

  4. 8 CRN-paired episodes per condition

  5. Plain English: The agents were evaluated across 8 episodes using Common Random Numbers (CRN).
  6. How CRN works: The starting positions of all agents and landmarks are set to be identical across two passes:
    • Condition A: Agents transmit and receive messages normally.
    • Condition B: Messages are zeroed out (silenced) via the counterfactual intervention do(messages = 0).

  7. Why it matters: In multi-agent navigation, random layout difficulty introduces huge variance (+/-300 to 400 reward noise). Because layouts are identical between Condition A and Condition B, environmental luck completely cancels out, leaving only the pure effect of communication.

  8. Communication benefit c = return with messages minus return with messages zeroed

  9. Plain Text Formula: Comm_Benefit c = Return_with_messages - Return_with_messages_zeroed

  10. Interpretation:

    • If c > 0: Communication improves performance. The agents actively use messages to coordinate.
    • If c = 0: Messages are ignored; communication is useless noise.
    • If c < 0: Messages are misleading or harmful.

  11. Seed is the unit of analysis; mean +/- SEM reported

  12. Plain English: Performance metrics were first calculated per seed (averaging its episodes), and then the mean and Standard Error of the Mean (SEM) were computed across the 5 seeds: SEM = Standard_Deviation / SquareRoot(N_seeds)
  13. Why it matters: Pooling raw episodes together would falsely inflate sample size; using the seed as the unit of analysis follows best statistical practices in reinforcement learning.

Takeaways Section Breakdown (Line by Line)

  1. Communication contributes positively on average in both settings.

  2. Meaning: Both 2a3l (+499.4) and 3a4l (+925.4) show positive communication gain, proving that MAPPO successfully learned an emergent protocol that improves cooperative navigation in both settings.

  3. The rise from 499.4 to 925.4 is not significant (exact two-sided permutation test, p = 0.246). It is a descriptive trend, not an established scaling relation.

  4. Plain English: Although the average communication benefit nearly doubled from 499.4 to 925.4 when adding more agents and landmarks, the variance across random seeds was large (e.g., Seed 2 in 3a4l learned very little comm benefit, while Seed 4 gained +1458.5).
  5. What the Permutation Test proves: An exact non-parametric permutation test checks whether the difference in means (+426.0) could happen by chance by shuffling the seed assignments. The result is p = 0.246 (> 0.05).

  6. Academic honesty: Acknowledging that this is an observed descriptive trend rather than claiming a mathematically proven universal law demonstrates scientific rigor.

  7. 4a5l (545.1) is an auxiliary scale point, excluded from the primary comparison.

  8. Meaning: The 4-agent, 5-landmark setting was evaluated as an exploratory benchmark on a single representative checkpoint rather than a full 5-seed matrix. Therefore, its comm gain (545.1) and action sensitivity (CIC_KL = 1.85) are reported as supplemental evidence, not lumped into the 2a3l vs. 3a4l statistical comparison.

3. Slide 2 Deep Dive: Testing 2 — Meta-Causal Decisions (3a4l)

Purpose of Testing 2

When autonomous agents are deployed in the real world, sensory drift or coordinate misalignment can distort communication meanings. Testing 2 evaluates our Meta-Causal Controller under an injected coordinate inversion (partner_full mirror). The controller must decide whether to trigger online parameter repair or abstain.

Definition of Table Metrics (Plain-Text Formulas)

  • Seed: Random seed of the trained 3a4l policy (Seeds 1 to 5).
  • dR (Reward Drop Percentage): dR = (Baseline_Return - Degraded_Return) / |Baseline_Return| * 100% Measures the percentage of task performance lost due to the environmental shift.
  • c_B (Baseline Communication Benefit): Communication gain before perturbation under uncorrupted conditions.
  • c_D (Degraded Communication Benefit): Communication gain after perturbation. If negative, messages actively reduce score compared to silence.
  • A_c (Causal Attribution Ratio, also called rho_causal): A_c = (Baseline_Comm_Benefit - Degraded_Comm_Benefit) / (Baseline_Return - Degraded_Return) Measures what fraction of the overall score drop is directly attributable to the collapse of communication.
  • S_c (Causal Evidence Score): A normalized composite score in the range [0, 1] integrating communication loss, policy KL collapse, and value sensitivity shift.
  • Decision: Repair (launch surgical LoRA fine-tuning) vs. Abstain (leave policy untouched and save compute).

  2. Demonstrates the two-tiered gating mechanism:
    • Tier 1 (Emergency Override): For catastrophic collapses (drop >= 50%), immediate intervention is taken.

  3. Seeds 2–4 abstained because task return fell less than the 10% minimum. Seed 3's large c_D arises because the no-message counterfactual performs very poorly under the shift; actual return fell only 3%.

4. Slide 3 Deep Dive: Testing 3 — Repair and Held-Out Results

Purpose of Testing 3

Once a repair is triggered, how is it performed, and does it actually generalize? Slide 3 shows results for our Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) module: tuning only 1,293 weights (<1.5% of the policy) for 15 PPO update iterations (<1% of retraining time).

Definition of Table Metrics (Plain-Text Formulas)

  • Local Recovery %: Local_Recovery % = (Repaired_Return - Degraded_Return) / (Baseline_Return - Degraded_Return) * 100% Percentage of lost performance recovered on the same diagnostic episodes.
  • Held-Out Recovery %: The exact same recovery formula re-evaluated on 20 fresh, unseen layout seeds (seed offset +1000). This tests whether the agent learned a genuine protocol repair or merely memorized the diagnostic episodes.
  • Clean Change %: Clean_Change % = (Post_Repair_Clean_Return - Baseline_Clean_Return) / |Baseline_Clean_Return| * 100% Evaluates catastrophic forgetting by placing the repaired agent back into the original clean, unshifted world.
  • Clean Task Retention %: Clean_Retention % = 100% - |Clean_Change %|

  1. Both LoRA repairs were locally accepted and stayed above the acceptance threshold on disjoint held-out layouts.
  2. Meaning: Our dual-acceptance rule requires at least 30% reward recovery and 20% communication recovery. Both repaired seeds easily cleared the threshold locally (76.7% and 80.4%) and maintained strong recovery on fresh, unseen layouts (64.2% and 68.2%), confirming genuine generalization.

  3. Why this matters: In ablation experiments, non-communicative motor tuning (noncomm_repair) passed local evaluation but completely collapsed on held-out layouts (-2.7% and -50.9% recovery). Testing on disjoint layouts is crucial to prevent deploying overfit policies.

  4. Clean-task retention was about 97.7% and 97.3% (about 97.5% mean).

  5. Meaning: Because LoRA adapts only 1,293 weights (<1.5% of parameters), the agents retained >97.3% of their original capabilities in the unshifted environment.

  8. Meaning: Recovery was not just raw navigation luck; the communication protocol itself was restored. Seed 5 regained 66.4% of its lost communication utility, while Seed 1's post-repair communication effect reached +16717.1, far exceeding its initial baseline.

5. Summary Cheatsheet for Presentations & Q&A

  • Q: Why is p = 0.246 in Slide 1 instead of p < 0.05?
  • Answer: The rise in average communication benefit from +499.4 to +925.4 is substantial, but across 5 seeds there is natural variance. We conducted an exact permutation test and honestly reported it as an observed descriptive trend rather than overclaiming an established scaling law.
  • Q: Why did Seeds 2, 3, and 4 abstain in Slide 2?
  • Answer: Because their overall reward dropped by less than 10% (Seed 2 dropped -9%, Seed 3 dropped 3%, Seed 4 dropped 6%). Updating weights when performance is essentially intact wastes compute and risks destabilizing a functioning policy.
  • Q: What makes LoRA better than full fine-tuning?
  • Answer: Full fine-tuning touches all 10,192 parameters and suffers from catastrophic forgetting (17.3% drop on clean tasks). LoRA tunes only 1,293 weights (<1.5% of policy) in 15 update iterations, achieving 78.6% recovery while preserving 97.5% clean task performance.
  • Q: Why is held-out validation necessary?
  • Answer: Diagnostic episodes can be overfit. In our ablation experiments, non-communicative motor tuning appeared to recover 40% on local diagnostic seeds, but when evaluated on fresh unseen layouts it failed completely (-2.7% and -50.9%). Disjoint held-out verification guarantees real-world generalization.
